RATING POLICY (#2)
For shelf scans, the backend calculation from complete, coherent nutrition values determines the color. GPT's different color no longer clears a usable rating. This does not establish that model-estimated nutrition is correct. Uncertain identity, missing required nutrients, implausible nutrition, conflicting observations and unfinished reviews retain their holds. A backend red rating cannot become green from a model opinion. The ordinary single-product Gemini prompt/flow is unchanged.

RANKING (#5)
Coherent products with previously blank model-disagreement ratings participate in equal-weight, category-specific comparison. Non-dominated green choices appear first, while nutrient trade-offs remain available. Dietary/care-plan restrictions still prevent suitability ranking. Optional mineral values are not treated as zero when missing, and explicit higher/lower priority directions are honored.
Outside-alternative requests wait for Finish and target at most one leading non-green product per category (up to three categories). A category with a green leading choice does not trigger an outside search. Existing alternative nutrition validation remains in place.

RECOVERY AND DIAGNOSTICS (#1)
Malformed food records are isolated by cell. Unreadable/unnamed uncertain cells do not discard valid neighbors. Associations remain strict: unknown or duplicate cell numbers are rejected rather than guessed.
One recovery dispatch per model maximum: only malformed/missing cells are requested again, or a whole failed dispatch is retried for an eligible transient error. Successful cells are retained and not reassessed. Explicitly unreadable products and missing nutrient values do not cause repeated requests. Cancellation/auth failures do not retry. Recovery still uses the original grid image and restricts the requested cell numbers; it does not create fresh crops.
Session and batch IDs, model, attempt, stage, safe failure code, affected cells and rating reconciliation are recorded as shelf_batch events. Final assembly failures are logged; one cell failure cannot discard its valid siblings. Diagnostics exclude raw image data, credentials and private health context.
If the phone loses the final stream after receiving product progress, it retains those facts as partial and unrated, continues later batches, and excludes unfinished results from ranking. Such progress cannot replace already completed results; later matching complete evidence restores eligibility.
