## Listing description

NutriGlimpse helps you understand food choices while you shop.

SCAN A PRODUCT
Photograph a package to identify it, view available nutrition information and see a simple rating with short reasons. Explore suggested alternatives and check USDA or Open Food Facts information when available.

COMPARE A SHELF
Use Shelf Camera to compare several packages. Results appear as products are processed, helping you explore the options in front of you.

YOUR PRIORITIES
Save dietary and food preferences in My Needs. Optional health context can inform general food guidance after you choose whether to share it with the AI providers.

YOUR STORE. YOUR OPTIONS.
Choose a grocery store or allow location while using the app. Suggestions can include store names and aisle information when available.

YOU CONTROL SHARING
Health sharing is optional. Review the app's privacy information and sharing choices before using photo or shelf recognition.

NutriGlimpse provides general nutrition information, not diagnosis, treatment or personalized medical advice. AI-generated identification, nutrition and ratings can be incomplete or incorrect. Check the package for nutrients, ingredients and allergens, and consult a qualified professional before medical decisions. Store and aisle suggestions do not guarantee availability.

Know your food. Understand your choices.
