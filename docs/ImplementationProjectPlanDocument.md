- New grid-based shelf requests have bounded backend model/cell/usage diagnostics. The earlier no-server-shelf-data statement concerned the pre-build-14 local flow; those older scans cannot be reconstructed retroactively. Raw photos are not retained.
- News collection is scheduled for **02:00 Eastern daily**, with missed-run/DST handling and bounded failure retries. All six feeds responded at the October 18 checkpoint; **EatingWell supplied 17 and ScienceDaily six eligible items**, while the other four supplied no stories within seven days. Connection does not guarantee daily output from every publisher.
- News selection sends only selected topic identifiers and applicable 65-plus/pregnancy audience flags. General or no useful interests sends only mode=general. Exact age, name, gender, weight, labs, location and photos are excluded. No news interests go to publishers or LLMs or into application news logs.
- Only general news is persisted to the public offline cache. A personalized selection can remain in memory during background/offline use; it is not promised to disappear on backgrounding. Profile changes invalidate the old selection. Reset returns through setup; the public general-news cache is not explicitly deleted. These are documented behaviors, not newly approved changes.
- Personal medical recommendations remain disabled. The October 25 single-product flow now supports explicitly consented shopper details for general food guidance; the new phone opt-in is included in build 20. Shelf health/lab transmission and BMI-derived clinical personalization remain disabled. News authorization remains separate.
