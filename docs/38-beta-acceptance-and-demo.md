# Beta acceptance and competition demonstration

Prepared October 14–15, 2026. This is a practical test/demo script, not a claim that a phone beta or competition submission is complete.

## First iPhone session

Bring the iPhone, confirm Apple membership is active, and use either authenticated Expo/EAS signing or a reachable Mac with Xcode. Install the source-matched signed build privately. Do not distribute the research detector models until their distribution rights are resolved; use the manual package/barcode workflow while that decision is pending.

1. Start without an account, read the actual retention agreement and inspect permissions. Declining optional permissions must leave manual flows usable.
2. Enter a fictional profile. Relaunch and confirm persistence. Make a change, export an encrypted archive and restore it with its password; verify wrong-password and duplicate-record behavior.
3. Scan three current packages, including original/reduced-sugar variants and an unreadable barcode. Verify exact name/variant/weight, unknown fields and source dates. Compare equal weights; see up to three alternatives and all nutrient trade-offs.
4. Open the guidance library offline. Change source topic/calorie table, inspect serving units and source links. No reference value should become a personal target automatically.
5. Exercise fictional product and receipt review: edit missing nutrition/quantity/date, exclude nonfood rows, confirm a catalog match, save, relaunch and delete. General real-media import remains disabled until its separate privacy acceptance.
6. For the already consented report route, begin with fictional printed reports: capture/pick/cancel, background during reading, relaunch after interrupted work, source-check every retained number/date and inspect actual outbound content. Only the allowlisted numeric/date evidence may leave the device. Original media must not remain in app caches.
7. At home and at a grocery store, test precise/approximate/denied location and neighboring branches. Confirm the selected store; retailer links must say availability is unknown.
8. If model rights permit the diagnostic build, test Shelf Scan with the same packages: pause/resume/background/finish/reopen; compare detected boxes with package identities; record misses and mistaken variants. Measure speed/heat on the actual phone.
9. Test large text, VoiceOver, landscape/tablet layout where applicable, airplane mode and poor cellular service. Save screenshots only with fictional data and appropriate permission.
10. Record build checksum/version, phone/OS, scenario, expected/actual result, severity and reproducible steps. Do not record private report contents or tester API secrets in shared issue notes.

## Suggested demonstration sequence

- Problem: grocery labels and health information are difficult to compare consistently.
- Scan or look up a real package, check the exact variant, show same-category alternatives at equal weight and explain the nutrient trade-offs.
- Demonstrate the store context and explicit availability-checking link without claiming live inventory.
- Show offline source-linked education and a fictional receipt review with an unresolved item.
- Show privacy through local storage, transparent report consent and deletion.
- Close with measured test evidence and honest boundaries: factual comparisons available; personalized medical recommendations await qualified review.
