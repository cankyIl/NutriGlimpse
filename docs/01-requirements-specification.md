# Nutrition and Health Shopping App — Requirements Specification

**Draft:** v0.20 — Real lipid import and beta retention agreement  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Status:** Implementation is underway in the authoritative remote workspace. The real lipid-import increment and its remaining device/clinical acceptance are recorded in [36-real-lipid-import.md](36-real-lipid-import.md); implementation evidence does not establish complete release readiness.  
**Companion:** [Design and Implementation Specification](02-design-and-implementation-specification.md)  
**Start here in a new chat:** [Session Handoff](03-session-handoff.md)

## 1. Purpose and decision status

The product is a polished iPhone and Android application that helps shoppers understand how grocery products fit their health conditions and dietary requirements. It recognizes products and labels, explains assessments using traceable evidence, recommends familiar alternatives, identifies the current store, reviews grocery receipts, and analyzes photographed or uploaded lipid reports. A website is planned later, using the same non-personal product and assessment services where practical.

This is a specification for the complete proposed release. Development may be sequenced, but sequencing does not remove any of the eight original capabilities or the subsequently requested lipid-report and no-login capabilities. The user explicitly rejected a merely useful first version and requested iterative specification review before implementation.

Each requirement carries one decision status:

- **Confirmed:** Explicit user instruction or decision. Its acceptance criteria may contain proposed details, which are labeled separately.
- **Proposed:** Recommended behavior or implementation-independent constraint awaiting review.
- **Open:** A choice that cannot yet be represented as an approved requirement.

“Confirmed” describes product intent, not technical feasibility, medical validation, third-party access, or delivery completion. No source listed in this document has endorsed this app or its scoring method.

## 3. Confirmed scope and interpretation boundaries

**Confirmed population and market:** United States; users enter their age; everyone aged 21 or older is in scope, with no upper age limit. Pregnancy is supported. English is the initial language, followed by Spanish and then additional languages in later enhancements. Users younger than 21 are outside this approved scope. Q-03 was resolved on October 4, 2026.

**Confirmed clinical coverage:** Type 2 diabetes/prediabetes, hypertension, high LDL/cardiovascular health, celiac disease, chronic kidney disease, fatty liver disease/MASLD, obesity/weight management, and SIBO. The user approved this catalog and confirmed the SIBO terminology on October 4, 2026 (Q-01/Q-02). IBS must not be silently substituted for SIBO.

Catalog and population approval establishes the required coverage; it does not validate individual clinical rules. Pregnancy-aware food assessments, alternatives, receipt assessments, and lipid-report interpretation must be included in the rule design and clinical review. Pregnancy support does not automatically add gestational diabetes or other obstetric diagnoses to the approved condition catalog. Required context, evidence and validation for pregnancy, older adults, SIBO and overlapping conditions remain part of Q-07.

The application provides food suitability information and understandable report analysis. It does not promise that one food causes, cures, or prevents a disease, predict an individual’s glucose or lipid response, diagnose from a photograph, prescribe medication, or replace an individualized clinical plan. These are **proposed interpretation boundaries** for review, rather than a reduction in requested functionality.

Scope is grocery foods and beverages, including fresh/unpackaged foods such as loose fruits, vegetables and meat. Where exact nutrition information is unavailable, use clearly identified estimates based on appropriate reference data. Restaurant meals, home-cooked recipes and supplements are excluded. Packaged grocery meals remain eligible when supported by product data. Specialized clinical diets and ambiguous grocery-prepared items still need applicability/handling review. Receipts identify excluded items without assigning unsupported nutrition judgments.

## 4. Functional requirements

### 4.1 Complete experience and platforms

**REQ-SCOPE-01 — Complete release [Confirmed].** Both mobile platforms shall offer all eight original capabilities, lipid-report analysis, local preferences/results, and operation without login. **Acceptance:** A release demonstration completes each journey on physical iPhone and Android devices. An unfinished journey cannot be represented by a placeholder or silently deferred to the website.

**REQ-SCOPE-02 — Website readiness [Confirmed direction; Proposed detail].** Shared services shall be reusable by a later website, while device-local personal data remains separate. **Acceptance:** Product knowledge and assessment contracts are documented independently of mobile screens. A future website must not automatically gain access to phone preferences or reports; transfer would require a new approved requirement.

**REQ-SCOPE-03 — Market and supported population [Confirmed; Proposed acceptance detail].** Support users in the United States aged 21 or older, including pregnant users and older adults without an upper age cutoff. **Acceptance:** Age 21 is included; age 20 is outside scope. Core journeys and applicable clinical rules are reviewed for the approved population, including pregnancy and overlapping supported conditions. Pregnancy cannot be excluded from the release by a generic unsupported-population message; uncertainty in an individual conclusion is handled under REQ-ASSESS-04.

**REQ-SCOPE-04 — Language roadmap [Confirmed; Proposed acceptance detail].** Launch in English, add Spanish next, and support further languages in later enhancements. **Acceptance:** The initial complete experience uses clear English, including errors, accessibility labels, source explanations and AI output. The design supports later localization without changing the device-only personal-data requirement. Spanish and additional languages are future enhancements, not initial-release acceptance conditions.

### 4.2 Health and dietary profile

**REQ-PROFILE-01 — Conditions and goals [Confirmed].** Users shall select diagnosed conditions, preventive goals, or both, and choose a primary concern. General Fitness and No Concern are also available without a disease selection; No Concern requires no selected health conditions, while General Fitness preserves any selected secondary conditions. **Acceptance:** The primary concern visibly affects explanation order; changing it does not erase other selections. Diagnosed and prevention contexts remain distinguishable in saved local settings.

**REQ-PROFILE-02 — Condition catalog [Confirmed membership; Proposed acceptance detail].** The app shall include all conditions listed in section 3, including obesity and SIBO. **Acceptance:** Every released condition has defined applicability, evidence, limitations, and supported assessment rules. Unreviewed conditions are not offered as fully supported; unresolved rule validation is a release dependency, not permission to silently remove approved conditions. Q-01/Q-02 are resolved; clinical review remains Q-07.

**REQ-PROFILE-03 — Dietary selections [Confirmed].** Provide vegetarian, include/exclude eggs, include/exclude dairy, halal, kosher, and gluten-free choices. **Acceptance:** Each setting can be saved locally and affects scans, receipts, and alternatives. Egg and dairy choices are independently editable. The app shall not equate excluding dairy with a diagnosed milk allergy or lactose intolerance.

**REQ-PROFILE-04 — Multiple constraints [Confirmed; Proposed acceptance detail].** Allow users to select multiple conditions and one primary concern. Assess all selected conditions and dietary requirements independently before ranking by the primary concern. **Acceptance:** A product favored for one condition cannot hide a conflict with another requirement. Show why no alternative satisfies the complete profile when applicable. The primary concern controls emphasis and ranking but does not permit disregarding other selected conditions or restrictions.

**REQ-PROFILE-05 — Evidence of compliance [Confirmed unknown-status behavior; Proposed evidence detail].** Halal, kosher and gluten-free status shall distinguish verified compliance, supported conflict and “Unverified” status. When a user requires one of these restrictions, a product with unverified status for that restriction is excluded from compatible alternatives. Certification and ingredient interpretation remain distinct. **Acceptance:** A scanned product can show Unverified without being mislabeled as a proven conflict. A vegetarian product is not automatically labeled halal or kosher; absence of an obvious gluten ingredient is not treated as verified gluten-free. Users can inspect the supporting evidence. Accepted evidence sources and certification authorities remain Q-08; unknown-status exclusion is approved.

**REQ-PROFILE-06 — Age, gender and pregnancy context [Confirmed age input and pregnancy support; Proposed collection detail].** Collect age as user input, ask optional gender (including Prefer not to say), and maintain relevant pregnancy context locally for applicable assessments. Gender does not infer pregnancy or clinical sex; legacy profiles without it remain valid. **Proposed detail:** Request age in years rather than a full date of birth; allow correction and periodically request an age update. Ask for pregnancy context explicitly rather than inferring it from appearance, a report or another preference. If context is not supplied, identify which conclusions require it. **Acceptance:** Changes create a new local profile revision; subsequent assessments use the updated context while historical results retain their original basis. Personal context follows the same transient-processing and device-only saved-data rules as other preferences.

### 4.3 Product, barcode, and label capture

**REQ-SCAN-01 — Product photo [Confirmed].** A camera image shall initiate recognition, ingredient retrieval, and nutrition retrieval. **Acceptance:** The result identifies the candidate product and variant, such as flavor, size, or formulation, with a confirmation path when ambiguous. Similar packaging cannot silently establish an exact match.

**REQ-SCAN-02 — Additional identification [Proposed].** Offer barcode capture and manual product search as alternatives. **Acceptance:** Users can switch methods without losing their current store or profile. A barcode with no catalog match leads to label capture rather than a fabricated record.

**REQ-SCAN-03 — Label fallback [Confirmed; Proposed capture detail].** If recognition fails or required information is missing, request additional photos. Request Nutrition Facts and ingredients/allergen statements when both are needed. **Acceptance:** The app explains the missing information, supports multiple photos, identifies blur/cropping, and permits correction of extracted text and serving units before assessment.

**REQ-SCAN-04 — Package versus catalog conflict [Proposed].** Newly photographed label facts and conflicting catalog facts shall remain distinguishable. **Acceptance:** The app shows material differences and asks which package is being assessed; it shall not combine nutrition from one variant with ingredients from another. Missing fields remain unknown, not zero.

**REQ-SCAN-05 — Capture failures [Proposed].** Camera permission denial, unreadable images, unsupported files, failed redaction, interrupted upload, no connection, and service timeout shall have recovery paths. **Acceptance:** Each error explains the next action, preserves permitted compact local state and active temporary input within the media-cleanup policy, and allows correction, cancellation or a retry consistent with REQ-LAB-08. A redaction failure cannot fall back to sending the original. Upload cancellation stops additional processing where technically possible.

**REQ-SCAN-06 — Fresh and unpackaged foods [Confirmed; Proposed acceptance detail].** Recognize and assess fresh foods as well as packaged products, including loose produce and meat. **Acceptance:** Distinguish a generic food reference from an exact branded-product match. Confirm ambiguous identity and relevant form/preparation, and request a portion or use an explicitly stated comparison amount. Nutrition estimates show their source and quantity basis; a photo does not establish exact weight, added ingredients or certification. If facts are insufficient, identify the missing information. Fresh foods do not enter a dead-end request for a nonexistent Nutrition Facts label. The same supported generic-food path applies to receipt items and food-type alternatives with honest retailer-evidence limits.

### 4.4 Food suitability assessment

**REQ-ASSESS-01 — Understandable categories [Confirmed labels; Proposed acceptance detail].** Assess suitability for the selected health context using “Good fit,” “Neutral,” “Limit,” and “Poor fit,” with separate “Restriction conflict” and “Insufficient information” states. **Acceptance:** The user can understand the conclusion and reason without opening a technical source. Missing evidence does not become Neutral, and a restriction conflict cannot be averaged into a favorable category. Label approval does not establish clinical cutoffs; reviewed mappings remain Q-07 and detailed presentation remains Q-12.

**REQ-ASSESS-02 — Evidence-linked reasons [Confirmed layered explanation; Proposed evidence detail].** Show a short explanation first and an expandable “Why?” section with nutrient details and sources. Each material assessment claim identifies the relevant nutrient or ingredient, portion basis, applicable rule and clinical source/version. **Acceptance:** Important conflicts and missing information remain visible in the summary rather than hidden in expanded details. The app never presents its own product-level category as a rating issued by ADA, AHA, NIH, KDIGO or another source organization.

**REQ-ASSESS-03 — Portion context [Proposed].** Distinguish per-serving, per-package, and comparison quantities. **Acceptance:** Changing the intended amount updates applicable calculations. Comparisons use equivalent quantities or explain their different serving sizes. The interface shall not imply that a receipt quantity is the amount consumed.

**REQ-ASSESS-04 — Clinical applicability [Proposed].** Apply rules only to their reviewed population and context across product, alternative, receipt and report assessments. **Acceptance:** User-entered age and declared pregnancy context inform applicability. Required context such as prescribed nutrient restrictions, disease stage, pregnancy details or dietary plan is requested or the affected conclusion is withheld. Unknown pregnancy context is not treated as not pregnant. No universal potassium restriction, protein target, or glucose prediction is inferred from a disease name alone.

**REQ-ASSESS-05 — Consistent reasoning [Proposed].** Identical verified facts, profile, portions, and rule versions shall produce consistent substantive conclusions. AI may explain the assessment but shall not invent thresholds or override verified restrictions. **Acceptance:** Repeated evaluations of the reference cases agree on category and conflicts; explanations contain no unsupported clinical assertion.

**REQ-ASSESS-06 — Uncertainty and symptom claims [Proposed].** Unknown identity, incomplete labels, uncertain certification, and limited evidence shall remain visible. SIBO symptom-compatibility claims must be distinguished from treatment or cure claims. **Acceptance:** Missing evidence never produces a “Neutral” or “Safe” default; unsupported assessment dimensions identify what additional information is needed.

### 4.5 Alternatives

**REQ-ALT-01 — Practical mainstream choices [Confirmed].** Suggest familiar products serving the same purpose and known to be sold by relevant major or regional retailers. **Acceptance:** Each specific suggestion has identifiable product evidence and retailer evidence. Generic invention, an unsupported store claim, or an obscure product selected solely for an attractive nutrition value fails acceptance.

**REQ-ALT-02 — Recommendation order [Confirmed].** Prefer suitable options carried by the current retailer, then suitable options at other retailers with nearby branches. Exact local stock is not required. **Acceptance:** Ranking records show that profile compliance and relevant improvement precede retailer preference. Nearby suggestions identify which retailer carries the product without claiming the branch currently has it.

**REQ-ALT-03 — Explain the tradeoff [Proposed explanation; Confirmed price exclusion].** State the specific improvement and any material downside at a comparable amount. **Acceptance:** An alternative cannot be described as globally “healthier” merely because one nutrient improved while a selected restriction worsened. Price lookup, price ranking and budget-setting controls are outside the planned scope. Provide recommendations using applicable health/dietary constraints, suitability and retailer availability; unavailable prices never block a recommendation. Receipt price parsing, if useful for interpreting a receipt, does not introduce price-based recommendations.

**REQ-ALT-04 — No suitable match [Confirmed; Proposed presentation detail].** If no verified product meets all selected restrictions, explain why and offer a wider store search without relaxing them. **Acceptance:** Users control radius expansion; the app does not loosen vegetarian, halal, kosher, gluten-free, egg, dairy or medical restrictions to fill the results list. If no match remains, show an honest no-match result. Any proposed food-type substitution is clearly labeled and subject to the same constraints.

**REQ-ALT-05 — Retailer coverage validation [Proposed].** Establish a representative evaluation basket for each named retailer before claiming supported coverage. A proposed starting basket is at least 50 distinct products per retailer across 10 everyday food categories, with relevant condition/restriction cases. **Acceptance:** Report product identification, verified retailer membership, eligible-alternative coverage, and unknown outcomes separately by retailer and category. Agree minimum pass thresholds through Q-09/Q-13 before release; a token catalog or aggregate success rate cannot establish adequate coverage for all six retailers.

### 4.6 Store and location

**REQ-STORE-01 — Current store [Confirmed].** With permission, use phone location to suggest the current grocery store and show its branch identity. **Acceptance:** The user can confirm or change it. Close-together branches, shopping centers, poor location accuracy, and movement create a clarification path rather than an invisible guess.

**REQ-STORE-02 — Manual operation [Proposed].** Location permission is optional. **Acceptance:** Users who decline it can select a retailer and search area manually and still scan products, review receipts, and get alternatives. Background location tracking is not required for any approved feature.

**REQ-STORE-03 — Nearby retailer guidance [Confirmed radius and ordering; Proposed implementation detail].** Show relevant nearby branches ordered by distance, with directions available, for alternatives unavailable in the current retailer's known catalog. Provide a user-adjustable radius defaulting to 10 miles. **Acceptance:** Store address, distance basis, active radius and directions are available; approximate distance is labeled. Driving time does not determine ranking. Radius changes update nearby results. With no relevant matches, offer expansion rather than silently changing the radius. Opening hours, if shown, have a provider source. Proposed implementation uses explicitly labeled approximate straight-line miles for search/ranking and saves the chosen radius locally; radius limits and directions integration remain engineering details under Q-11.

**REQ-STORE-04 — Availability wording [Confirmed].** Catalog evidence is sufficient; live stock is outside the requirement. **Acceptance:** Use wording such as “Sold by this retailer; local availability may vary.” “In stock here” is prohibited without separate reliable stock evidence. Lack of a listing is described as “No verified listing found,” not proof that the retailer does not carry it.

### 4.7 Receipt analysis

**REQ-RECEIPT-01 — Receipt capture [Confirmed].** Accept a receipt photograph, identify food lines, and assess recognized foods for the local profile. **Acceptance:** Mixed receipts separate food, non-food, discounts, taxes, and uncertain lines. A receipt with no food returns an informative completed result.

**REQ-RECEIPT-02 — Abbreviations and ambiguity [Proposed].** Resolve shortened item names using retailer context and available product identifiers. **Acceptance:** Exact matches, candidate matches, and unresolved items are distinguishable. The user can correct a match or photograph the package. A receipt description alone cannot establish ingredients, certifications, or a precise formulation when ambiguous.

**REQ-RECEIPT-03 — Basket feedback [Confirmed intent; Proposed detail].** Present item-level benefits/concerns and useful swaps, with a concise basket overview. **Acceptance:** Feedback reflects matched purchases and uncertainty, does not diagnose the shopper, and does not infer that all purchases were consumed by one person. Refunds, duplicate lines, quantities, and weighted items have explicit handling.

**REQ-RECEIPT-04 — Partial completion [Proposed].** Long, folded, faded, cropped, duplicate, and multi-image receipts shall support correction or partial results. **Acceptance:** The interface shows analyzed, unresolved, and excluded item counts. A partially read receipt cannot appear fully verified, and failure on one item does not discard successful matches.

**REQ-RECEIPT-05 — Store context for swaps [Proposed].** Receipt alternatives shall default to the confirmed retailer on the receipt, with an explicit option to choose a current shopping retailer instead. **Acceptance:** Uploading a historical receipt at home or in a different store does not silently change its same-retailer recommendation context. Nearby-search origin is displayed and can be changed.

### 4.8 Lipid-report analysis

**REQ-LAB-01 — Optional AI-assisted input [Confirmed; current implementation documented].** Users may photograph a lipid report or import a report image or PDF at any time. Providing a panel is optional and never a prerequisite for product scans, food ratings, alternatives or receipt analysis. **Acceptance:** The journey requires no consumer login, explains external processing before submission and requires the current initial beta agreement. The implemented native path reads the report locally, reconstructs only supported measurements, units, comparison signs, page numbers and test date, and sends that typed evidence to OpenAI for an exact structured consistency check. It does not send original or redacted page images, original PDFs, filenames, or arbitrary OCR text. JPEG, PNG and unlocked PDFs are bounded to 20 MiB and ten pages. This increment does not implement original-pixel LLM extraction or the full clinical interpretation target; see [current evidence and limitations](36-real-lipid-import.md).

**Confirmed availability:** Users can add a report at any time during normal app use, including after skipping it initially or after providing earlier reports. **Proposed navigation:** Keep an “Add lipid report” action accessible from My needs and lipid-report history, alongside the report capture mode. No repeat onboarding, product scan or account is required. Cloud processing still requires connectivity; offline behavior follows REQ-UX-03.

**Confirmed input boundary:** No standalone manual entry of lipid values. A report photo/image or PDF is required for the lipid workflow. Users confirm the extracted values before they affect personalization or any clinical interpretation under REQ-LAB-02. Source-linked correction is implemented for the current report import; it does not permit creating a panel without a report.

**REQ-LAB-02 — Structured extraction and confirmation [Confirmed confirmation; Proposed extraction/recovery detail].** Extract analyte names, numerical values, units, test date, reference ranges and report flags when present. Show the extracted values for user confirmation before the one-time clinical analysis, including when extraction appears clear. **Acceptance:** Interpretation does not begin until confirmation. Missing values, illegible characters, inequality signs and ambiguous units are flagged; the system never supplies an assumed LDL, fasting status or unit as if printed. Source-linked correction or reimport is proposed for mistakes. This confirmation follows automatic redaction and extraction; it does not introduce a redaction-approval screen or standalone manual panel entry.

**REQ-LAB-03 — Separate report and interpretation [Proposed].** Distinguish the laboratory’s printed reference ranges/flags from any guideline context or personalized clinical target. **Acceptance:** A report’s “high” flag is attributed to the report. Guideline explanations cite their source and applicability; a laboratory reference interval is not automatically presented as the individual’s treatment target.

**REQ-LAB-04 — Bounded analysis [Proposed].** Explain supported lipid measurements, uncertainty, and relevant nutrition context in plain language without diagnosis or prescribing. **Acceptance:** Interpretation uses reviewed evidence applicable to age and declared pregnancy context, and identifies missing context that prevents a conclusion. Unusual or concerning results receive a clinician-reviewed follow-up message where defined; the app does not recommend starting/stopping medication or claim a definitive cardiovascular risk calculation from an incomplete report. No clinical cutoffs are invented in this specification.

**REQ-LAB-05 — Compact local results [Confirmed; Proposed representation].** Save extracted information and analysis as compact structured app data only on the user's phone. Do not retain original report PDFs/photos or their image derivatives as app attachments after processing; the source file remains with the user. **Acceptance:** Completed results remain viewable offline and supply future personalization without another report-analysis call. Store necessary measurements, units, dates, concise findings, evidence references and version/provenance fields without embedding images/PDFs, a full OCR transcript or provider conversation in the analysis record. Preserve meaningful precision and uncertainty. Remove temporary report copies, rendered pages, redacted images and previews when no longer needed, including cleanup after cancellation/failure and recovery from interrupted sessions. The user's source in Photos/Files is untouched. Measure actual result-storage size on representative reports; an exact byte budget is Q-13. No durable analysis result belongs in app-operated services. OpenAI may retain submitted evidence and output under the default provider policy explicitly accepted under REQ-PRIVACY-04; the app must not claim provider-wide non-retention. An unsuccessful request must not create a falsely completed local report. Temporary-copy cleanup mechanics remain proposed; retained source-report attachments are not an option in the approved scope.

**REQ-LAB-06 — Automatic food personalization [Confirmed; Proposed acceptance detail].** Confirmed lipid-panel results shall automatically inform future food ratings and product alternatives, without a separate personalization opt-in. Select the most recent confirmed panel by test date, not upload date. Providing a panel remains optional. **Acceptance:** With no confirmed panel, the app evaluates products using selected conditions and dietary requirements. Confirming a newer panel updates future assessments and alternative rankings through reviewed rules; uploading an older panel later does not replace the newer one. The same engine applies to newly assessed receipt items and swaps. Unconfirmed extraction cannot affect recommendations. Show the active panel's test date and explain relevant influence without requiring every rating to change. Lab findings must not erase dietary restrictions, other condition constraints or the user's primary concern, or create a diagnosis automatically. Saved results and derived personal context remain device-local. Missing/ambiguous test dates and conflicting same-date reports remain Q-04; clinical freshness rules remain Q-07, and deletion details remain Q-06. Do not silently substitute upload date or blend values from different panels.

**REQ-LAB-07 — Untrusted document content [Proposed].** Text in images, reports, receipts, product descriptions and AI outputs shall be treated as data, not executable instructions. **Acceptance:** A malicious document asking the model to ignore rules, reveal secrets, send data elsewhere or change a profile cannot trigger those actions. Unexpected output fails validation and offers correction or user-initiated recovery under REQ-LAB-08; it is not saved as a successful analysis.

**REQ-LAB-08 — Reuse completed analysis; allow failed-attempt retry [Confirmed; Proposed mechanics].** Analyze a report once successfully, save the result and reuse it. Users may retry a failed attempt when no successful result was saved, including an uncertain outcome after a connection failure. **Acceptance:** Reopening, restarting, food scans, alternatives and recognized duplicate imports do not reanalyze a report with a saved successful result. Initial extraction and interpretation of confirmed values are distinct workflow steps. Check saved-result and in-flight state before each attempt; fingerprints stay on the phone. If a valid result remains in memory after a save failure, retry the local save first. A user-requested retry after an uncertain connection outcome can repeat remotely completed computation; this possibility was explained and accepted. Do not claim exactly-once provider execution. Retry permission does not authorize automatic repeat analysis of saved results. Corrections and loss/deletion of saved records still require defined engineering recovery behavior.

**REQ-LAB-09 — Supported lipid measurements [Confirmed scope; Proposed extraction detail].** Support total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C and triglycerides, plus ApoB and Lp(a) when present. **Acceptance:** Recognize supported measurement names and retain their reported units, values, flags and provenance; missing measurements remain missing. Applicable interpretations use reviewed rules, including population/context limits. Do not infer an absent additional test or invent unit conversions. Non-HDL-C handling when reported or transparently derived remains a proposed clinical-rule detail for review; this approval does not expand the feature to unrelated blood tests.

### 4.9 Shelf Scan mode — approved October 14, 2026

Implementation detail: [Detailed Shelf Scan implementation specification](26-shelf-scan-implementation-spec.md). The user will test on iPhone; physical-iPhone acceptance is required. Shared TypeScript plus a Swift native capture/preprocessing module is the recommended engineering approach, not a wholesale platform rewrite.

Shelf Scan is a toggleable continuous aisle-scanning mode. It supplements individual product scans. Its transient lifetime is an explicit exception to persistent food-scan history requirements.

**REQ-SHELF-01 — Continuous category capture [Confirmed; Proposed mechanics].** While active, repeatedly capture shelf images, identify food products and accumulate distinct products as the user moves along the aisle. Compare within one category using the existing health profile, dietary restrictions and most recent confirmed lipid context when available. **Acceptance:** Cereals, cheeses and cookies can each form category-specific scans; products need not remain in view to remain in the current ranking. Original and reduced-sugar versions belong to the same category but remain distinct product entries. Proposed handling groups sizes of identical formulations into one entry; category suggestion/correction remains a design detail. User-selected sampling target: 2 images per second (one every 500 ms) while actively scanning under normal conditions. Verify physical-iPhone capture timestamps and separately measure actual completed identification throughput; reduced processing pace must not be hidden by a growing queue or a capture-FPS claim.

**REQ-SHELF-02 — Personalized top ten [Confirmed; Proposed ranking detail].** Continuously show up to ten best-fitting identified products within the scan category. **Acceptance:** Use all selected constraints, equivalent comparison quantities and traceable facts/rules; a favorable primary-condition score cannot hide a restriction conflict. Known conflicts and unverified required restrictions are excluded from the suitable top ten and explained separately. Fewer than ten eligible products yields a shorter list, not fabricated entries. Rankings are relative to assessed products seen in this scan, not every item on the shelf or in the store. Exact tie-breaking and clinical ranking mappings remain reviewed engineering/clinical work.

**REQ-SHELF-03 — Uncertain products without interruption [Confirmed].** Collect uncertain matches under Needs confirmation without stopping the scan to ask about each one. **Acceptance:** Review is available when paused or finished. Only sufficiently identified products with enough nutrition/ingredient evidence enter the ranking; missing evidence is not a healthy default. Correcting a candidate recomputes the affected category ranking. No forced top-ten placement for an unresolved match.

**REQ-SHELF-04 — Explicit controls [Confirmed; Proposed inactivity detail].** Provide Start scan, Pause, Resume and Finish scan with a visible scanning status/product count. **Acceptance:** Finish stops capture and leaves results available; it does not delete the scan. Holding the camera still does not imply completion. Switching away pauses capture. Prolonged inactivity may prompt Still scanning? and pause, but cannot silently finish or delete results; the threshold remains an engineering detail.

**REQ-SHELF-05 — Retain all scans until app closure [Confirmed; Proposed process-lifetime mechanism].** Starting another scan preserves previous Shelf Scan runs for the duration of the current app session. Clear Shelf Scan runs when the app is closed/terminated; do not create persistent scan history. **Acceptance:** Scan A remains accessible after starting and finishing scan B. Leaving a scan screen, changing mode, finishing capture or starting a new scan does not clear earlier runs. Backgrounding pauses capture and preserves runs while the app remains alive; a fresh launch after termination starts with no prior Shelf Scan runs. Proposed implementation keeps these runs in memory only, excluded from the local database, restoration state, exports, backups and backend persistence. Do not depend on a shutdown callback to delete a durable copy. Captured frames remain temporary processing media. This exception does not delete saved individual-product/receipt history, lipid reports or preferences.

## 5. Privacy and data requirements

**Approved export exception:** Device-only persistence remains the default. The user approved optional, explicitly initiated encrypted export of saved results for backup or phone transfer, with a user-controlled file/destination. This exception for user-managed copies does not authorize app-operated cloud storage or automatic synchronization. The separately accepted OpenAI retention exception is described in REQ-PRIVACY-04. These two explicit exceptions qualify device-only statements throughout these specifications.

**REQ-PRIVACY-01 — No account [Confirmed].** No login, account creation, or identity-linked cloud profile is required. **Acceptance:** Fresh installations complete every approved journey without registering or supplying login credentials. This does not imply anonymous network traffic or anonymized report contents. The app has no automatic cross-device synchronization of personal preferences or results.

**REQ-PRIVACY-02 — Device-only persistence [Confirmed location; Proposed safeguards].** Personal preferences, selected conditions, saved analysis, and extracted lipid data shall persist on the device only. Encrypt sensitive app data using platform-supported protected storage. **Acceptance:** Storage inspection verifies protection at rest; network/log inspection finds no persistent personal profile or results in app-operated services. Shared product data must not carry user health or location identifiers.

**REQ-PRIVACY-03 — Transient processing [Proposed mechanism].** An app-operated backend may process the minimum personal context needed for a request without retaining personal payloads. **Acceptance:** Request bodies, images, model prompts/responses, precise locations, and extracted health data are excluded from persistent logs, caches, queues, traces, analytics, and error attachments. Cleanup and failed-request behavior are verified. The separate accepted OpenAI default-retention exception is documented in REQ-PRIVACY-04 and Q-05; it does not authorize backend retention.

**REQ-PRIVACY-04 — OpenAI retention boundary [Confirmed October 14 beta decision].** The user accepts OpenAI default API retention through a mandatory initial beta agreement, version `beta-report-ai-2026-10-14.1`. The app backend must still avoid storing personal request content or results. **Acceptance:** Agreement is saved locally before beta use; opening or declining the agreement sends no report. Optional report processing discloses the exact limited fields sent and links the [OpenAI API data policy](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data). `store: false` does not remove all provider retention: default abuse-monitoring content retention may be up to 30 days, with applicable exceptions and other feature-specific retention. Zero Data Retention approval is not a prerequisite for this accepted beta arrangement and is not claimed. Deleting local records does not delete provider-retained content. Verify actual configured API features, outbound content and disclosure consistency; do not infer anonymity from removed identifiers.

**REQ-PRIVACY-05 — Local controls and backups [Confirmed storage boundary; Proposed controls].** Exclude personal app records from automatic cloud backup under the current device-only requirement. Provide deletion of individual reports/history and all local personal data. Define the exclusion mechanism, device migration, export, reinstall, and lost-phone behavior. **Acceptance:** Deleted records disappear from active app storage and subsequent assessments. No guarantee of physical flash erasure is implied. Exclusions must be verified through Q-06; enabling cloud backup would require a later explicit change to the storage requirement. Source images previously stored in Photos/Files may already participate in user-configured cloud synchronization outside this app; app deletion cannot promise removal of those originals or external copies.

**Shelf Scan exception:** REQ-SHELF-05 governs continuous shelf runs: retain all runs in memory during the app session, preserve them when another scan starts, and clear them on app termination. They are excluded from persistent history and encrypted exports. The following saved-result rules apply to ordinary product scans, receipts and lipid results.

**Confirmed saved-result retention:** Food-scan results, receipt analyses and lipid results stay in local app storage until the user deletes them; no automatic age-based removal. **Proposed acceptance detail:** Do not silently evict saved results to make space. Explain low-storage failures and offer user-directed deletion. This policy covers saved structured results, not temporary photos/PDFs, which must still be cleaned up under REQ-LAB-05 and REQ-PRIVACY-10. Retention does not guarantee recovery after app deletion, device loss or storage failure, and does not require using outdated clinical guidance.

**REQ-PRIVACY-06 — Permission and minimization [Proposed].** Request camera, photo, and location access only when relevant. Send only necessary context; do not require names, email addresses, or full medical history. **Acceptance:** Permission refusal leaves documented manual routes. External disclosures identify what image/context leaves the device and for which service, including location used for place lookup.

**REQ-PRIVACY-07 — Operational metadata [Open; Proposed short-lived handling].** Define permitted retention of request-specific network and anti-abuse metadata separately from prohibited persistence of personal content. A proposal is short-lived session tokens and in-memory rate-limit counters with explicit expiry, plus aggregate metrics. **Acceptance after Q-14:** Every token, IP-derived counter, request identifier, hosting log, and provider metadata record has an approved purpose and retention policy. No hidden persistent identifier becomes an anonymous-user profile.

**REQ-PRIVACY-08 — Automatic redaction before every LLM request [Confirmed; Proposed mechanism].** Automatically remove names, SSNs and address information identifying people from all content before transmission to an LLM, including the first extraction call, analysis, explanations and any retries. No mandatory redaction preview or user approval step shall be added. This covers report photos/PDFs, receipts, incidental identifiers in product images, text fields, filenames and metadata. **Proposed detail:** Perform detection and irreversible masking on the phone; send only sanitized derivatives. The implemented real-report path instead uses local OCR and strict allowlist reconstruction: only supported numeric measurements, units, comparison signs, source-page numbers, test date and enumerated issue codes may leave the phone. All other OCR text and all pixels are excluded. OpenAI checks the structured evidence and cannot introduce or repair values; original-image LLM extraction remains unimplemented. The proposed masked-image path for other workflows still needs its own validation. Remove identifying document names/addresses conservatively when their role is uncertain; public product/retailer identities used for recognition are a distinct data category. **Acceptance:** Inspect actual outbound bytes. Original pixels, hidden PDF text, annotations, attachments, metadata or copied text cannot carry the removed identifiers. Failure or unresolved redaction uncertainty blocks transmission of that content and offers recapture, selection of a clearer file or cancellation; omitting user review does not permit sending an unsanitized original. No external LLM receives unredacted data to perform the redaction. Automated detection needs validation. The separate confirmed clinical-value review under REQ-LAB-02 is unaffected.

**REQ-PRIVACY-09 — Encrypted export and restore [Confirmed scope; Proposed mechanics].** Allow users to export saved results, selected conditions, primary concern and dietary preferences to an encrypted file they control for backup or phone transfer, and restore it locally. **Acceptance:** Export/import is explicitly initiated, with no account, automatic cloud sync, backend storage or LLM processing. Include compact results, the approved profile/settings and necessary provenance/completed-analysis state; exclude all user-provided photos/PDFs and their derivatives, temporary files, app credentials and provider conversations. Use reviewed encryption with a portable user-held secret; exact format/key UX remains engineering design. Validate integrity/schema before importing and do not rerun completed report analyses. Explain that exported copies remain user-managed after in-app deletion. Restore collisions and further export mechanics remain Q-06; inclusion of selected conditions and dietary preferences is approved.

**REQ-PRIVACY-10 — Compact dated results without retained photos [Confirmed; Proposed date/cleanup detail].** Retain only compact extracted product/receipt results with dates, not their photos, thumbnails or image derivatives. **Acceptance:** Save the identification, relevant nutrition/ingredients, receipt line facts and concise assessments needed for history and reassessment without embedding images or full provider transcripts. Record capture/import time; keep the receipt's printed date separately when available and never invent it from the import date. Report test dates remain governed by the lipid requirements. Remove app-owned processing media on completion, cancellation/failure or interrupted-session cleanup. Existing originals in the user's Photos/Files remain untouched. Saved structured results stay until deletion; exported backups exclude photos/PDFs. Exact data-size budgets remain engineering work, preserving meaningful precision and provenance.

A general food database is never described as a disease-scoring authority.

## 7. Experience and operational quality

**REQ-UX-01 — Polished mobile experience [Confirmed objective; Proposed measures].** Provide coherent visual design, responsive layouts, clear hierarchy, readable explanations, guided capture, useful loading states, and actionable errors. **Acceptance:** Both platforms pass the complete journey review, including permission denial, ambiguity, missing data, offline states, and cancellation. **REQ-UX-02 — Accessible, respectful feedback [Proposed].** Support platform screen readers, large text, adequate contrast, touch targets, and non-color indicators. Use neutral language about food, body weight, and uncertainty. **Acceptance:** Core journeys are operable without relying on color, tiny text, or visual recognition of an unlabeled control. Accessibility checks use an agreed standard and device matrix.

**REQ-UX-03 — Responsive and offline behavior [Proposed targets].** Show interaction feedback within 300 ms on reference devices; aim for a cached product result within two seconds and an ordinary online scan assessment within 15 seconds at the 95th percentile under defined test conditions. Longer receipt/report requests show progress and cancellation. **Acceptance:** Benchmark conditions and request sizes are documented; locally saved preferences and completed results remain usable offline. Targets require Q-13 approval.

**REQ-OPS-01 — Versioned releases and recovery [Proposed].** Track app, data, model configuration, and rule versions independently; support withdrawing an incorrect rule or data record. **Acceptance:** A faulty rule can be disabled without deleting local user data, and results identify when reassessment is available. A rollback exercise demonstrates restoration of the last approved behavior.

**REQ-OPS-02 — Privacy-preserving operations [Proposed].** Diagnose reliability and cost using aggregate operational metrics without retaining personal content. Protect third-party credentials outside the app bundle. **Acceptance:** Credential inspection and log inspection pass; abuse controls do not require an account or persistent health profile. Provider failure yields honest partial/unavailable states.

**REQ-OPS-03 — Release quality gate [Proposed].** Release only after functional, evidence, privacy, accessibility, and platform verification is complete. Proposed targets: zero unresolved critical/high-severity defects, all mandatory end-to-end cases passing, and 100% traceability for material health claims in the reviewed case set. These are release targets, not claims about achieved performance or universal clinical accuracy.
